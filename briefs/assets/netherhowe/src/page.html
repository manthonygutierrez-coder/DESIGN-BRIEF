New wraps from the week of 16 February 2027 Notice to the trade and to display operators

The reel width changes and the second ink goes. Every tube leaving here from that week carries a redrawn wrap. Old two-colour wraps stay in circulation alongside them until the stock is gone, probably into 2028. The statutory information on both is identical and nothing about the contents has changed.
