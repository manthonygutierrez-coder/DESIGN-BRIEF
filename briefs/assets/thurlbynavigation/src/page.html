HomeThe Nettlebed Flight

Nineteen miles of navigation from Thurlby Basin to the junction at Wardlow End, eleven locks of it in the last two and a half. The Trust owns the water, the gates and the towpath, and is answerable for all three still being where you left them.

Notice to navigators The Nettlebed flight is closed from 2 November to 26 March

All eleven locks come out for hydraulic gear. There is no passage through the flight in that time and no way round it — the nearest alternative is forty-one miles by way of the Ouse. Winter moorings above lock one are free to anyone caught on the wrong side of the closure, and we would rather you took one than tried the Ouse in February.

Stoppage and works notices

18 September Gauge boards renewed, locks 3 and 9

Both read 30 mm high against the old ones. The figures painted on the chamber wall are wrong and are being ground off.

11 September Hydraulic gear: contractor appointed

Work starts at the foot on 3 November and moves up the flight one lock a fortnight.

2 September Side pond at lock 9 to be filled

Out of use since 1971 and holding water it should not. Filling begins once the flight is drained.
