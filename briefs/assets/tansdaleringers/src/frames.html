Thirty-one towers, and somebody to teach in nineteen of them.

HomeTowers

The Guild exists to keep bells ringing in the Tansdale deanery, which in practice means finding, teaching and keeping the people who ring them. Thirty-one towers are on the books. Nineteen have a band able to ring on a Sunday, and that figure has not gone up since 2019.

St Botolph’s: the ring comes out on 5 January

The eight are being augmented to ten. The bells come out on 5 January, the frame is extended through February, and the whole ten are dedicated at 11:00 on 25 April. There is no ringing at St Botolph’s in that time. Practice moves to the parish room, Tuesdays at 19:30, on the dumb bell and the simulator. The room holds twelve and we are already turning people away, so write before you come.
