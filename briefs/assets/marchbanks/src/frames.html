MARCHBANK’S AMUSEMENTSTravelling amusements since 1911. Ghost train, waltzer, two juveniles.
HomeWhere we are

Six lorries and two generators, wintered in a yard at Woodbarrow and on the road from Easter to the first week of October. Four rides, three of them older than anyone now driving them.

The Old Mill will not open before Easter

All eleven figures came out of the tunnel after the inspection on 28 September and have been burnt. The ride travels this winter as a shell, the doors chained, and it does not take money until there is something behind them. The waltzer and the juveniles run as booked.

A figure is passed at between 1.2 and 1.8 seconds depending on where it sits on the loop. Nobody gets a second look at anything in here.
